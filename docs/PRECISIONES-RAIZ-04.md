# Quantity measurement rules

Measurement lines retain dimensions, supported formulas, comments and subtotals. Calculated quantities should be edited through their source lines. Unsupported combinations are reported, and certification measurements remain separate from budget measurements.

This note follows the current product scope. For the maintained English guide, see [Quantity measurement rules](CALCULATIONS.md). Use the documentation supplied with an older release when reviewing that build; capabilities and validation may differ.
