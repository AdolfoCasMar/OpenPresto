# Procurement, issued documents and balances

Suppliers, competing offers, contracts and purchase documents are separate from customers, issued documents and receipts. Imported documents remain drafts until reviewed. Due states without payment dates do not create historical cash movements, and proformas are not treated as actual invoices.

This note follows the current product scope. For the maintained English guide, see [Procurement, issued documents and balances](USER-GUIDE.md). Use the documentation supplied with an older release when reviewing that build; capabilities and validation may differ.
