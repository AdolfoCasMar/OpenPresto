# Certification and progress periods

Certification can be stored at origin or by period, with separate quantities and prices. Supported detailed lines are checked against period quantities. Calendar approval is explicit, and missing dates remain unknown.

This note follows the current product scope. For the maintained English guide, see [Certification and progress periods](USER-GUIDE.md). Use the documentation supplied with an older release when reviewing that build; capabilities and validation may differ.
